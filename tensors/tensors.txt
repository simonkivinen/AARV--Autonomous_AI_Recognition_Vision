Video
these are nothing more than a series of images so tensors of size three that are stacked together in a very specific sequence
of what we call frames. These frames and code change in the pixel across time and their
ordering is very important for the understaning of the video data.
There for a video tensor input to a newralnetwork can be seen as having five dimensions three for the image
or what we call frame one more for a stack of image for a specific video or a subsection of a video and
finally another dimension for a batch of these video to feed into the neural network as a mini batch
The neural network type for a video can take many form but it's usually a modified form of vision based network
that leverage the sequential aspect of the batch of data being feed to it.
